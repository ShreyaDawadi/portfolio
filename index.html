§ 01/About

Web applications, and the networks beneath them.

I am a Computer Engineering student at the School of Engineering, Pokhara University, where I have been studying since 2023. Most of what I build is full-stack web: React in the browser, Node.js and Express behind it, PostgreSQL underneath, taken all the way from schema to deployment.

In 2026 I spent three months as a Networking Intern at Nepal Telecom, designing, configuring and testing enterprise networks with VLANs, OSPF, DHCP and ACLs. It left me with a habit I now bring to software as well: isolate a fault one layer at a time, and write down what was designed, configured and tested.

On the software side my work centres on database design, authentication and API development. Beyond the web, my projects reach into mobile and augmented reality, including PocketLab AR, my final-year project.
